## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. [Customer Satisfaction Prediction — Olist E-commerce · Capstone](https://github.com/Rajat-Singh-Source/olist-customer-satisfaction)
`Python` `Pandas` `LightGBM` `SHAP` `LIME`

Merged **9 relational tables** into a leakage-safe, order-level dataset of **95,823 orders** and
built a LightGBM classifier that catches **72.5% of unsatisfied orders (0.715 ROC-AUC)** — so teams
can act before bad reviews arrive. SHAP/LIME identified delivery delay, delivery time and seller
history as the main drivers, leading to proactive delay alerts and closer seller monitoring.

### 2. [Namma Yatri — Ride Demand & Cancellation Analysis](https://github.com/Rajat-Singh-Source/namma-yatri-ride-analysis)
`Python` `Pandas` `SQL` `Power BI` `Tableau`

Integrated **5 relational tables** with Pandas and showed that only **45.5% of bookings complete**,
with cancellations split almost evenly between customers (**48.2%**) and drivers (**47.3%**).
Identified peak hours and high-performing zones to guide operational improvements.

### 3. [CardioRisk — Cardiovascular Disease Prediction](https://github.com/Rajat-Singh-Source/cardiorisk-cvd-prediction)
`Python` `scikit-learn` `EDA`

Cleaned **70,000 patient records → 68,648 valid rows** with physiological validity rules and
engineered BMI/age features. Compared Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest;
the tuned tree reached **AUC 0.773** with the train–test gap cut from 36 points to **1.7 points**.

### 4. [NYC Yellow Taxi — Operations EDA](https://github.com/Rajat-Singh-Source/data-analytics-portfolio/tree/main/04-nyc-taxi-eda)
`Python` `Pandas` `Matplotlib` `Seaborn`

Analysed **~1.89M trips** of 2023 yellow-taxi data: fixed schema drift across monthly files,
imputed missing values, and profiled demand, fare-per-mile, tipping and zone imbalances to
recommend routing and dispatch changes.

### 5. [Bank Data Analysis — PySpark ETL](https://github.com/Rajat-Singh-Source/data-analytics-portfolio/tree/main/05-bank-etl-pyspark)
`PySpark` `Pandas` `Seaborn`

Built a Spark ETL pipeline over global banking data — currency-normalised market capitalisation,
top-10 rankings, quartile segmentation and market-concentration analysis.

### 6. [Used Car Price Prediction — Regression](https://github.com/Rajat-Singh-Source/data-analytics-portfolio/tree/main/06-car-price-regression)
`Python` `scikit-learn` `Ridge/Lasso`

Regularised regression on used-car listings reaching **test R² ≈ 0.92**, with alpha-path and
coefficient analysis used to control overfitting and explain price drivers.
